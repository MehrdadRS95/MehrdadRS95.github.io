Research

Research & publications

My research focuses on securing LLM-powered agents, particularly the Model Context Protocol (MCP). I study staged trust attacks, adversarial context, and defense strategies across agent-tool boundaries, building on earlier work in time-series forecasting and applied machine learning.

Current focus

Doctoral research directions

Staged Trust Attacks on MCP Servers

Investigating how malicious MCP servers can establish trust through benign interactions before shifting their behavior, and evaluating defenses against these temporal attacks.

Adversarial Context & LLM Robustness

Benchmarking the robustness of LLM-powered agents to adversarial context across Model Context Protocol layers, with a focus on cross-boundary threats and security evaluation.

Defense Placement & Threat Coverage

Mapping security controls to the MCP attack surface and studying which threats different defense placements can mitigate, along with gaps in their coverage.

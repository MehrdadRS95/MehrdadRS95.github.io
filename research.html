Research

Research & publications

My work spans AI security for emerging agent protocols and applied machine learning for forecasting and demand prediction — from adversarial defense taxonomies to meta-learning across forecasting algorithms.

Current focus

Doctoral research directions

AI Agent & MCP Security

Systematizing where defenses should sit across the Model Context Protocol attack surface, and analyzing coverage against agent-level threats.

Adversarial Robustness

Hardening ML and LLM-based systems against adversarial inputs, prompt injection, and evasion — from threat modeling to automated defense.

Defense Placement & Coverage

Building taxonomies and coverage-analysis frameworks that make defense trade-offs explicit and measurable for practitioners.

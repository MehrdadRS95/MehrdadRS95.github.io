About

Bridging AI security research and production data science

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at Old Dominion University, where my doctoral research focuses on AI security, adversarial robustness, and defense strategies for emerging AI agent protocols. I recently co-authored MCP-DPT, a defense-placement taxonomy and coverage analysis framework for Model Context Protocol security.

Before my PhD, I spent six years as a data scientist and ML engineer — enhancing predictive trading models, deploying real-time streaming pipelines, and building BI dashboards that drove product and market decisions. I care about systems that are both rigorous and shippable: models that hold up under adversarial pressure and pipelines that run reliably in production.
